Our Roots

The Historic Soul of South Masi Street

Madurai has been celebrated as the textile capital of Southern Tamil Nadu since the ancient Pandyan era. In 1985, Hanifa Textiles opened its doors near the landmark Vilakkuthoon with a simple philosophy: never compromise on yarn count, fabric softness, or fair pricing.

From pioneering the distribution of certified Viking, Poomex, and Kambit cotton nighties to stocking pure Ramraj temple wear and Raymond formal textiles, our showroom has grown into a cherished family staple for festivals, weddings, and everyday ease.
